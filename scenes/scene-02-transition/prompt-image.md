# Cena 02 — Prompt de imagem

Create a conceptual transition still for a premium law firm website.

This image should feel like a natural continuation of the hero scene.
Use the warm light from the window as a bridge between scenes.
The mood should begin in the physical world and subtly suggest a transition into a more contemporary and digital legal context.

Visual ideas:
- elegant light wash
- refined interior atmosphere
- subtle graphic lines or reflections
- sense of continuity from the first scene
- strong editorial composition
- room for large typography

Do not make it futuristic or tech-heavy.
The visual should feel sophisticated, minimal and connected to the previous scene.
