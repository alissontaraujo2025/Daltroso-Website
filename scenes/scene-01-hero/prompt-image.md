# Prompt de imagem — Cena 01 Hero

Create a premium cinematic website hero image for a sophisticated Brazilian law firm.

Scene: an elegant contemporary office interior during late afternoon, viewed from table height. A refined dark navy ceramic coffee cup and saucer sit on the right side of a natural dark wooden table. Very subtle steam rises from the coffee. Large architectural windows are visible in the background, softly out of focus. Warm natural sunlight enters through the windows and creates long, soft geometric shadows across the room and table.

Composition: preserve generous clean negative space on the left half of the frame for large editorial typography. The coffee cup must remain on the right third. Keep the center visually calm so the composition can support a website interface.

Mood: intelligent, calm, sophisticated, trustworthy, human, editorial, cinematic, understated luxury.

Lighting: realistic warm window light, soft contrast, natural highlights, no artificial glow, no fantasy lighting.

Camera: full-frame editorial photography, 50mm lens look, shallow but controlled depth of field, realistic perspective, high detail, refined color grading.

Materials: realistic ceramic, natural wood, glass, subtle fabric and architectural surfaces.

Important: no people, no legal gavel, no scales of justice, no courthouse columns, no text, no letters, no logos, no watermark, no floating objects, no excessive decorative elements.

Aspect ratio: 16:9.
